About this event

What do we do at Cleanup at Blossoms?

Twice a month, volunteers gather at Blossoms Park to roll up their sleeves and keep our community beautiful. From picking up litter to tidying up green spaces, every pair of hands makes a huge difference.

The main cleanup session kicks off at 3:00 PM, but feel free to arrive anytime between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM. Gloves, bags, and supplies are provided — just bring your energy and a love for keeping Blossoms looking its best!

What to expect

  • Litter pickup and general park tidying throughout the day
  • Main cleanup session starts at 3:00 PM — all hands on deck!
  • Gloves, trash bags, and supplies provided on-site
  • Family friendly — a great activity to bring kids along
  • Drop-in anytime between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM
