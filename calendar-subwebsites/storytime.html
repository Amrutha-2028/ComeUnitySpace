About this event

What do we do at Story Time?

Story Time is a fun weekly gathering for toddlers and young children held every Wednesday at Blossoms Library. Join us for a cozy hour of read-alouds, songs, and movement activities designed to spark a love of books and learning.

Whether you're a longtime Blossoms resident or brand new to the neighborhood, Story Time is a wonderful way to meet other families, give your little ones a chance to socialize, and build early literacy skills in a warm, welcoming space.

What to expect

  • Interactive read-aloud stories for toddlers and young children
  • Singing and movement activities to get the wiggles out
  • A welcoming space for caregivers to connect with one another
  • Drop-in format — no registration required, just show up!
  • All ages welcome, though activities are designed for ages 0-5
