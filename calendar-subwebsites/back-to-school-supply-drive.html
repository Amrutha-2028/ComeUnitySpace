About this event

Help Kids Head Back to School Ready!

Every August 2nd, our community comes together at Daisy Park to make sure every child heads back to school with the supplies they need to succeed. The Back to School Supply Drive collects donated school supplies all day long to distribute to students in need right here in our neighborhood.

Every pencil, notebook, and backpack makes a real difference in a child's confidence and ability to learn. Stop by anytime throughout the day to drop off your donations and help set our kids up for a great school year!

What to expect

  • Drop off new or gently used school supplies at donation tables
  • Most needed items: backpacks, pencils, notebooks, crayons, and folders
  • Volunteer opportunities available to help sort and organize donations
  • A warm, community-driven atmosphere at Daisy Park all day
  • Drop-in format — stop by anytime throughout the day
