About this event

What happens at Spring Gardening?

Every March 24th, Blossoms Park comes alive with the spirit of spring! Our Spring Gardening event is a beloved community plant and seed swap where neighbors bring their extras and leave with something new to grow at home.

Whether you're a seasoned green thumb or just getting started, this is a wonderful opportunity to trade plants, discover new varieties, share gardening tips, and connect with fellow plant lovers in the community. Come dig in with us!

What to expect

  • Plant and seed swap — bring extras from your garden to trade
  • Tips and advice from experienced community gardeners
  • A relaxed, friendly atmosphere perfect for all skill levels
  • Great for families — kids love getting their hands in the soil!
  • Drop in anytime from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM
