About this event

What happens at the Summer Kickoff?

Every June 2nd, Meadow Park becomes the hottest spot in the neighborhood as we kick off summer in the best way possible — with water balloon fights, water gun battles, fresh fruit, cool drinks, and pure community fun from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM!

Come dressed to get wet and ready to have a blast! Whether you're a kid or a kid at heart, the Summer Kickoff is the perfect way to celebrate the start of the season with your neighbors and make unforgettable memories together.

What to expect

  • Epic water balloon fights — come ready to get soaked! 💦
  • Water gun battles for all ages
  • Fresh fruit and cool drinks to keep you refreshed all day
  • Tons of laughs and community fun from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM
  • Free and open to all — wear clothes you don't mind getting wet!
