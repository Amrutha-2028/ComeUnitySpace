About this event

Share the Feast This Thanksgiving!

Every November 25th, our community gathers at Blossoms Park to make sure no neighbor goes without a Thanksgiving meal. Our Thanksgiving Food Drive collects food donations and brings volunteers together all day long to pack food trays for families in need right here in our neighborhood.

Thanksgiving is all about gratitude and togetherness — and what better way to celebrate than by giving back to those around us? Drop off a donation, stay to help pack trays, or simply come show your support. Every act of kindness counts!

What to expect

  • Drop off non-perishable food donations at collection tables
  • Help volunteers pack Thanksgiving food trays for families in need
  • Most needed items: canned goods, stuffing, cranberry sauce, and pasta
  • A warm, grateful community atmosphere all day long
  • Drop-in format — stop by anytime throughout the day
