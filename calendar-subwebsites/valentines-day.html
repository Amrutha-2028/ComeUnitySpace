About this event

Spread the Love at Daisy Park!

Every February 14th, Daisy Park is filled with love, laughter, and sweet treats! Bring your friends, family, or that special someone for a day of heartfelt fun — from decorating heart-shaped cookies to crafting handmade cards, there's something for everyone to enjoy.

Whether you're celebrating with a partner, your kids, or your best friends, our Valentine's Day event is all about showing appreciation for the people in your life and connecting with your community. Come make some sweet memories with us!

What to expect

  • Heart-shaped cookie decorating for all ages
  • Handmade Valentine's card crafting station
  • A festive, love-filled atmosphere at Daisy Park
  • Great for families, couples, friends, and solo visitors alike
  • Free and open to all — drop in anytime from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
