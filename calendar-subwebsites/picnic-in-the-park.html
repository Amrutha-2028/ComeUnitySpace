About this event

What happens at Picnic in the Park?

Every May 11th, Daisy Park transforms into a beautiful community potluck! Bring a dish of your choice, lay out a blanket, and spend a relaxed morning connecting with neighbors old and new over great food and great company.

Whether you bring a homemade casserole, a bag of chips, or your grandma's secret recipe — all contributions are welcome and celebrated. Just bring your appetite, a blanket to sit on, and an open heart ready to make new friends!

What to expect

  • Community potluck — bring any dish you'd like to share!
  • Bring a blanket or lawn chairs to sit and relax on the grass
  • Laid-back atmosphere perfect for chit-chatting and making new friends
  • Great for families, couples, and solo neighbors alike
  • Free and open to all — drop in anytime from 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM
