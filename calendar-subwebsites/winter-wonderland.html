About this event

What happens at Winter Wonderland?

Every year on December 28th, Blossoms Park transforms into a magical Winter Wonderland! From fun seasonal activities to amazing food vendors, this is one of the most beloved events of the year for families and neighbors to celebrate the holiday season together.

Come enjoy a full day of winter festivities from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Whether you're looking to make memories with your family, grab something delicious from our vendors, or just soak in the cozy winter atmosphere — this is the place to be!

What to expect

  • Fun winter activities for all ages throughout the day
  • Amazing food vendors serving seasonal treats and warm drinks
  • A festive atmosphere perfect for families and friends
  • Live entertainment and community performances
  • Free and open to all — no tickets required, just show up!
