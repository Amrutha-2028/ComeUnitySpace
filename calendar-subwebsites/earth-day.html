About this event

Celebrate Our Earth at Blossoms Park!

Every April 22nd, we come together at Blossoms Park to celebrate and honor our planet! Earth Day at ComeUnity Space is a joyful mix of creativity and action — from crafting art out of recycled materials to rolling up our sleeves for a community cleanup.

This is a great opportunity to teach kids about sustainability, connect with eco-minded neighbors, and make a tangible difference in our community — all while having a great time. Come celebrate the Earth with us from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM!

What to expect

  • Art-making station using recycled and upcycled materials
  • Community cleanup of Blossoms Park and surrounding areas
  • Fun eco-education activities for kids and families
  • A festive, planet-loving atmosphere with fellow community members
  • Free and open to all — drop in anytime from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM
