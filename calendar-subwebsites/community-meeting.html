About this event

What happens at the Community Meeting?

Every Monday, neighbors gather at Blossoms City Hall for our weekly Community Meeting — a space where residents come together to share ideas, raise concerns, and shape the future of our neighborhood.

The meeting includes an open planning session focused on organizing upcoming festivals, events, and initiatives. Whether you want to volunteer, pitch a new idea, or simply stay informed, everyone's voice is welcome here.

What to expect

  • Open discussion on neighborhood news and upcoming events
  • Planning session for festivals, drives, and community initiatives
  • Opportunities to volunteer and get involved in local projects
  • A welcoming space for all residents — new and longtime alike
  • Drop-in format — no registration required, just show up!
