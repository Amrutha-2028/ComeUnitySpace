About this event

What do we do at the Food Drive?

Every Wednesday at Blossoms Park, our community comes together to pack food trays and donate non-perishable goods for neighbors in need. It's a simple but powerful way to make a real difference for families right here in our community.

Whether you bring a bag of groceries or stay all afternoon helping pack trays, every contribution counts. Come for an hour or stay the whole day — all help is welcome and deeply appreciated!

What to expect

  • Drop off non-perishable food donations at the collection tables
  • Help volunteers pack food trays for families in need
  • Meet your neighbors and give back to the community together
  • All ages welcome — a great activity for families with kids
  • Drop-in format — come and go as your schedule allows
