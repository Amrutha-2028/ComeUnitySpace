About this event

Welcome Autumn at Blossoms Park!

Every September 25th, Blossoms Park fills with the warm sights, smells, and sounds of fall for our beloved annual Fall Fair! From delicious food stalls to fun activities and live music, this is a full day of autumn magic that the whole community looks forward to every year.

Breathe in that pumpkin-spiced air and spend the day wandering through stalls, enjoying great food, listening to music, and soaking up the best that the fall season has to offer — all right here at Blossoms Park with your neighbors!

What to expect

  • Food stalls serving seasonal autumn treats and favorites
  • Fun fall activities for kids and adults throughout the day
  • Live music to set the perfect fall atmosphere
  • A festive, pumpkin-filled setup throughout Blossoms Park
  • Free and open to all — come and go as you please all day long!
