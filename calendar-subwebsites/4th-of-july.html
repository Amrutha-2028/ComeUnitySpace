About this event

Celebrate Independence Day at Blossoms Park!

Every July 4th, Blossoms Park lights up with patriotic pride and community spirit! Join us for a full morning of Independence Day festivities — from fun 4th of July activities to a community parade that brings the whole neighborhood together.

Come dressed in your red, white, and blue and celebrate the holiday the ComeUnity way — surrounded by neighbors, laughter, and the joy of being part of something bigger than ourselves. From 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Blossoms Park is the place to be!

What to expect

  • Fun 4th of July themed activities for all ages
  • A community parade through Blossoms Park — join the march!
  • Patriotic decorations and a festive Independence Day atmosphere
  • Great for families — bring the kids and grandparents!
  • Free and open to all — wear your red, white, and blue!
