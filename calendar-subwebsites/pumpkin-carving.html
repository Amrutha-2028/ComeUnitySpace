About this event

Show Off Your Best Pumpkin!

Every October 23rd, Meadow Park gets spooky and creative for our annual Pumpkin Carving Challenge! Bring your own pumpkin from home or grab one of ours and compete against your neighbors to see who can carve the most impressive jack-o'-lantern.

Whether you're a seasoned carving pro or trying it for the first time, this is a great excuse to get into the Halloween spirit early and spend a fun afternoon with your community. Carving tools are provided — all you need to bring is your creativity!

What to expect

  • Pumpkin carving challenge — bring your own or use one of ours!
  • Carving tools and supplies provided on-site
  • Show off your finished jack-o'-lantern and compete for bragging rights
  • Spooky, festive atmosphere perfect for getting into Halloween mode
  • Fun for all ages — families, kids, and adults all welcome!
