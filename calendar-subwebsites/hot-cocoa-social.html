About this event

What happens at the Hot Cocoa Social?

Kick off the new year with warmth and good company! Every January 13th, neighbors gather at Blossoms Park for our cozy Hot Cocoa Social — a morning filled with steaming mugs of hot cocoa, delicious toppings, and plenty of friendly conversation.

Whether you're a longtime resident or brand new to the neighborhood, this is the perfect low-key event to meet new people, catch up with old friends, and start the year feeling connected to your community. Come as you are and stay as long as you like!

What to expect

  • A hot cocoa stand loaded with goodies and toppings to customize your cup
  • Warm, welcoming atmosphere perfect for meeting new neighbors
  • Cozy winter vibes at Blossoms Park from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM
  • Great for all ages — bring the whole family!
  • Free and open to all — just show up and enjoy!
