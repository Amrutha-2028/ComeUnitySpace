About this event

What happens at the Environmental Board Meeting?

Every Saturday at Blossoms City Hall, our Environmental Board meets to discuss ways to make our community cleaner, greener, and more sustainable. From organizing cleanups to planning eco-friendly initiatives, this is where real change starts.

Whether you're passionate about the environment, want to help plan community cleanups, or simply want to learn how you can make a difference locally — all are welcome to attend and share their voice.

What to expect

  • Discussion on upcoming cleanups and environmental initiatives
  • Planning sessions to make Blossoms a cleaner, greener place
  • Opportunities to lead or join local sustainability projects
  • Open to all residents — no experience or background needed
  • Drop-in format — no registration required, just show up!
