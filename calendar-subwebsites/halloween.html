About this event

Trick or Treat at Blossoms Park!

Every October 31st, Blossoms Park comes alive with spooky fun for the whole family! Put on your best costume and join your neighbors for an evening of trick-or-treating, games, and Halloween festivities from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

Whether you're bringing little ghouls and goblins for their first trick-or-treat experience or just love the Halloween spirit, this is the perfect community celebration to get into the spooky season with friends and neighbors!

What to expect

  • Trick-or-treating throughout Blossoms Park
  • Costume contest — show off your best look!
  • Spooky games and activities for all ages
  • Halloween treats and goodies for the kids
  • Free and open to all — costumes encouraged but not required!
